A little about me

I’m a Business Intelligence Analyst passionate about turning data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions. My experience includes working with SQL Server, T-SQL, Power BI, Power Query, Excel, and DAX to extract, transform, analyze, and visualize data. I enjoy taking raw or messy datasets, preparing them for analysis, building meaningful KPIs, and creating dashboards that make complex information easier to understand. Before transitioning into Business Intelligence, I worked as a Junior Database Administrator, where I gained experience with database performance, query optimization, access management, views, stored procedures, and database maintenance. This background gave me a strong foundation in understanding how data is stored, processed, and accessed. Today, I focus on the analytical side of data, working with stakeholders to understand business requirements, develop reporting solutions, and turn data into actionable insights. I’m continuously expanding my skills in data analytics, business intelligence, data modeling, SQL, Power BI, and AI/ML, while building practical projects that demonstrate how I approach real-world data problems. Core Skills: SQL Server • T-SQL • Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Excel • Data Cleaning • Data Modeling • KPI Development • Data Visualization • Business Intelligence
