Stellar Mart Sales Comparison Analysis

Power BI SQL Excel DAX

Compared sales performance across three branches of a supermarket chain using 1,000 transactions from January to March 2019. Cleaned the data in Excel, validated it in SQL, then built an interactive Power BI report with drillthrough pages and five custom DAX KPIs. Found a February dip at every branch, a stalled Store B (+0.5% growth against +26.1% at Store A) and a membership program driving just over half of revenue.

Evergreen Health Center Operations & Billing Analytics

Power BI SQL Excel DAX

Built a healthcare analytics suite for a hospital network dealing with appointment no-shows, billing disputes, and uneven doctor workloads. Modeled relationships across appointments, revenue, doctors, and departments, then built a three-page Power BI suite — Operations, Billing, and an AI Insights page with a natural-language Q&A visual and a decomposition tree for tracing revenue problems back to their root cause.
